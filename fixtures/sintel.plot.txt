Sintel is a young woman with short red hair who travels alone, carrying a spear, in search of a dragon.

After the opening credits over snowy mountains (nothing happens on screen before about 27 s), Sintel fights a single old man in the snow: one fight against one opponent, not a battle against several.

She crosses snowy mountains and meets an old man who tells her she is a fool to travel alone. When he asks who she is looking for, she answers: a dragon.

Part of the film goes back to when she found a small, hurt dragon. She cared for him and named him Scales. He is small and pale, and he stays close to her. They spend their days together in a town of tiled rooftops. One day a much larger dragon swoops down and carries Scales away.

Sintel sets out to find him. Her search takes her over rooftops, through ruins, across a desert, into a bamboo forest and down into dark caves lit by glowing crystals, where she has to fight with her spear.

The end credits run over the last stretch of the film; there is nothing to describe there.
